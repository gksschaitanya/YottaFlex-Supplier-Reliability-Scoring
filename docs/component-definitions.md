# Component metric definitions

The score component table stores numerator, denominator, raw observation count, eligible observation count, excluded count, status, explanation, and component metrics JSON. Each definition below describes the required measure; implementation gaps are explicitly noted.

| Component | Numerator | Denominator | Unit / eligible population | Minimum observations | Missing treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|
| Delivery | Eligible PO lines whose cumulative delivered quantity reaches ordered quantity by required date | Valid non-DRAFT, noncancelled PO lines due within the inclusive scoring window and known at cutoff | PO-line count; never shipment-count or quantity weighted | 5 | No eligible observations is insufficient/missing; invalid or historically unknown status lines are excluded with reasons |
| Quality | Accepted quantity | Valid inspected quantity | Quantity units; completed, nonvoid inspections | 3 inspections and 100 units | Zero units is missing, never 0% |
| Cost | Sum of eligible PO-line scores | Count of eligible PO lines | Equal-weight PO-line observations; invoice quantities are combined only within the same PO line/UOM | 5 PO lines | Invalid, unreconciled, post-cutoff, duplicate, voided, unsupported-status, and incomparable-currency rows are excluded with evidence reasons |
| Capacity | Total gross receipt quantity allocated once, capped by shipped and committed quantities | Total eligible committed quantity | Eligible supplier/site/product/UOM/period commitments; commitments receive equal denominator weight by quantity | 3 commitments | Zero commitments is missing; invalid commitments are excluded with reasons |
| Compliance | Passed applicable completed checks | Passed + failed applicable completed checks | Check count; valid evidence and effective requirement | 5 checks | Missing/expired evidence is not pass |
| Disruption | Total severity-weighted exposure (lower is better) | Configured exposure scale | Positive-duration, valid, de-duplicated signals overlapping the half-open window | No separate positive minimum; an empty source is missing data | No source records known by cutoff is `MISSING_DATA`; invalid-only sources are `EXCLUDED_INVALID_DATA` |

Disruption exposure is severity weight × fractional active days × `0.5^(signal age in days / 90)`. Duration is clipped to the intersection of `[starts_at, ends_at)` (or open-ended through `as_of`) and `[window_start, as_of)`. Zero-duration and out-of-window signals are excluded. Exact duplicate references are the same nonblank `(source, source_reference)` pair; null references are not deduplicated. Different references are counted independently even if their periods overlap. Event and recorded timestamps must both be no later than the scoring cutoff. The score is `max(0, 100 × (1 - exposure / exposure scale))`, rounded HALF_UP to two decimal places. Evidence is emitted from the same in-memory calculation result as component metrics.

### Cost measure definitions

- **Variance:** `abs(invoice unit price - PO unit price) / PO unit price`. A variance up to 2% scores 100; 20% or more scores 0; the interval declines linearly. Variance and per-line scores are calculated with `BigDecimal`, `HALF_UP` to 12 decimal places; the component score is rounded `HALF_UP` to two places.
- **Invoice aggregation:** each PO line is one eligible observation. Valid invoice-line scores against that PO line are weighted by their eligible invoiced quantity to form that PO-line score. Invoiced quantity is consumed in invoice-date, invoice-business-ID, and invoice-line-ID order and is capped at ordered quantity. Partial invoices count only their accepted quantity; excess quantity is excluded. The eligible PO-line scores are then averaged with equal weight, so no quantities from different PO lines or UOMs are added.
- **Component metrics:** numerator is the sum of eligible PO-line scores; denominator is the number of eligible PO lines. Observation and excluded counts are PO-line group counts. `invoiceLineObservationCount`, `eligibleInvoiceLineCount`, and `excludedInvoiceLineCount` separately report invoice-line evidence row counts.
- **Comparability:** invoice currency must equal PO currency. Product and UOM are resolved through the invoice line's required PO-line reference; invoice lines do not independently store product or UOM, so an independent invoice-vs-PO product/UOM comparison is not possible in this schema.
- **Reconciliation:** line amount must equal quantity × invoice unit price rounded `HALF_UP` to four decimal places within 0.0001. Invoice subtotal must match the sum of its invoice-line amounts, and header total must match subtotal + tax + freight within 0.0001. Tax and freight do not affect unit-price variance. Invoice discounts, credits, and surcharges are not modeled.
- **Duplicate/over-invoice policy:** repeated nonblank external invoice source references are treated as duplicate invoices. The schema also enforces unique invoice business IDs and unique non-null source references. Multiple distinct invoices against one PO line are treated as partial/overlapping invoices, but their eligible aggregate quantity is capped at ordered quantity. If source reference is null, semantic duplicate invoices cannot be identified from this schema alone.
- **Historical policy:** only `MATCHED` and `APPROVED` invoices can contribute. An invoice whose `updated_at` is after `as_of` is excluded because current status cannot be reconstructed at the historical cutoff. Invoice, invoice-line, PO, and PO-line creation timestamps must all be at or before `as_of`; if any line on an invoice was created after the cutoff, the whole invoice's subtotal contents are treated as unknown at cutoff. There is no invoice status-history table.

### Capacity measure definitions

- **Fulfilment source:** gross `receipt_line.received_quantity`; accepted inspection quantities are not used because quality is evaluated independently.
- **Allocation:** receipt lines are deduplicated by stable receipt-line ID and processed by receipt timestamp, receipt ID, and line ID. Cumulative receipt quantity is capped at the linked shipment-line's shipped quantity. Each remaining receipt quantity is allocated at most once to commitments matching supplier, site (exact for a site-specific commitment; null commitment site means any valid supplier site), product, UOM, and receipt timestamp in `[period_start, period_end + 1 day)`. Allocation priority is site-specific first, then earliest commitment end, earliest start, and commitment ID. Partial receipt quantities can be split across commitments.
- **Quantity caps:** allocation cannot exceed remaining receipt quantity, remaining shipped quantity, or remaining commitment quantity. Receipt quantity above shipped quantity is excluded from fulfilment and exposed as `excessReceiptQuantity`; quantity with no matching commitment or no remaining commitment capacity is `unallocatedReceiptQuantity` with a reason.
- **Formula and metrics:** numerator is total allocated fulfilled quantity; denominator is total eligible committed quantity. Score is `100 × numerator / denominator`, capped at 100. The metrics include eligible/excluded commitment counts, total committed/allocated/remaining quantity, utilization percentage, unallocated and excess receipt quantities, allocation count, duplicate receipt-line count, and reason counts. Eligible commitment minimum defaults to 3.
- **Historical cutoff:** commitment creation/recorded timestamps, receipt creation/receipt timestamps, receipt-line creation, shipment and shipment-line creation, and PO and PO-line creation must be no later than `as_of`. Receipts before the scoring window start are excluded even if a commitment period began earlier. Metrics and evidence use the same allocation result; source rows beyond the cutoff are filtered from the operational query.
- **Schema limitation:** there is no direct capacity-commitment-to-PO-line foreign key. Matching uses the defined business dimensions; the schema cannot prove which individual PO line a commitment intended to cover.

### Delivery measure definitions

- **On-time-in-full (OTIF):** numerator is eligible PO lines whose cumulative quantity from shipments with valid `DELIVERED` events reaches the ordered quantity by the required date. Denominator is all eligible due PO lines. The score is `100 × numerator / denominator`.
- **Completed on-time rate:** completed eligible PO lines with completion on/before required date divided by all completed eligible PO lines.
- **Completed late rate:** completed eligible PO lines with completion after required date divided by all completed eligible PO lines.
- **Partial delivery quantity:** delivered quantity, capped at ordered quantity, on eligible lines still incomplete at `as_of`.
- **Overdue outstanding quantity:** ordered quantity less delivered quantity (floored at zero) for eligible lines still incomplete at cutoff. Eligible lines are due by cutoff, so all remaining quantities are overdue.
- **Eligible, cancelled, and other excluded line counts:** PO-line counts. Cancellations are counted separately; other exclusions include drafts, not-yet-due/out-of-window lines, invalid lines, status unknown at cutoff, and final-status/quantity mismatches.

`OPEN` and `PARTIALLY_FULFILLED` lines are scored from delivery evidence. `FULFILLED` and `CLOSED` lines are eligible when their delivered quantity reconciles to ordered quantity; otherwise they are excluded as inconsistent source data. `DRAFT` and `CANCELLED` lines are excluded. If PO `updated_at` is after the run cutoff, the current mutable status is not used to infer the historical status; the line is excluded as status unknown at cutoff. There is no PO status history table. Shipment status is not treated as historical delivery proof; valid delivery events are authoritative.

Shipment lines are read separately from delivery events, deduplicated by stable shipment-line ID, and grouped once by shipment. Multiple delivered events for one shipment do not multiply shipment quantity. For multiple valid delivered events, the latest event occurrence is used as that shipment's conservative delivery timestamp; this can delay a completion, but adding an event cannot improve it. Cumulative delivered quantity across shipments determines the line completion timestamp. Event occurrence/recording, shipment creation, shipment-line creation, PO creation, and PO-line creation are bounded by the scoring cutoff.

Component observation counts and quantity metrics remain separate units. The PostgreSQL integration assertion compares persisted delivery evidence counts and OTIF contributions to the component numerator, denominator, and metrics. The service-level unit tests exercise the calculation; PostgreSQL behavior still requires a fresh dedicated database run.
# Compliance fields

The compliance component metrics include applicable, mandatory, and critical requirement counts; supplier/site scope counts; passed, failed, pending, expired, and missing-evidence counts; excluded records; eligible observations; and coverage. The component status and score are persisted from the same calculation result that generates evidence. Mandatory critical failures, pending assessments, expired assessments, and missing evidence are reported as escalation reasons; non-mandatory critical failures remain visible without mandatory escalation.
